## TPF Reclassification (v6.16)

`TPF_MASTER.md` and `TPF_MODULE_*.md` are project-specific (dental Business OS), not a universal layer. Target location — `docs/artifacts/reference/tpf/`, not `roles/`. Universal module direction — `TEMPLATE_MODULE_DEV.md` §2; business minimum — `DOMAIN_STANDARDS.md`. For new projects, TPF files are not created by default.

## Artifact Layout — wave-first (v6.16)

Wave artifacts (DEV_PROMPTS, QA_REPORT, VISUAL_QA_REPORT, DESIGN_SPEC, MOTION_CONCEPT, MICRO_SPEC) go together into `docs/artifacts/waves/[N]/`. Cross-cutting (BUSINESS_LOGIC, BUSINESS_ROUTES, SPINE, DEVELOPMENT_PLAN) — in `docs/artifacts/` root. Registries — `decisions/`, `roadmap/pass1·2/`. Render baselines — `frontend/tests/visual/__baseline__/`.
